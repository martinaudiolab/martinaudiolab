My Philosophy

Martin Audio Labs is a company I built from the ground up as a single person operation. Every single aspect of this company is controlled by me, and meets my personal standards. Every tool, every component, and every process has been tailored to my needs, standards, and quality. I control all aspects of Martin Audio Labs, and in reality this company is merely an extension of me.

Restorations to me are not just a functional service performed to make something more reliable. I take pride in my work and units do not leave my home unless I am personally satisfied with the end result. Many shops specialize in high output, quickly shuffled restorations as they are focused primarily on profit and moving things as quickly as possible means more routine profit generated. I do not focus on moving things as fast as possible, I focus on doing things right and I am not satisfied unless you are.

My desire to do things right has caused me to branch out and build on a multiplicity of skill sets typically divvied up to different people. I offer complete woodworking options, I develop circuit boards to solve engineering hurdles where appropriate, and I rebuild all the boards. In fact, the earliest Martin Audio Labs ventures were typified by producing replacement parts for other techs, my earliest venture being a replacement for the venerable BA312 IC found in Sansui and Marantz products. This wide range of applied skills gives me some advantages that other shops fundamentally lack. In fact, I regularly sell my own products to my competitors.

I also do not see restorations as just a reliability improvement. I firmly believe, through my experience of rebuilding many, many units, that a proper high quality restoration makes a noticeable improvement in sound quality. I reject many of the assertions pushed today about the superiority of vintage parts. I believe we today have the best parts that are available, and if you choose them right you will improve the sound of a vintage piece, not harm it. I wholeheartedly embrace and believe in the technology available to us today to deliver repeatable, noticeable improvements in sound quality, and I believe a unit should sound better leaving than when it entered my home. Every single modern high end audio products embraces new technology, and so should high end restorations.
